# The Sumunbléd Chronicles

A pixel-art, tile-scrolling medieval RPG. Anything you have seen up close can be summoned in battle as a sword made of that very thing, with a random special ability.

Project manager: Kimball. Built with Claude.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a web browser. No install needed.

- Move: arrow keys or WASD (on-screen pad on touch screens)
- Talk, read signs, open chests: walk into them
- Enter buildings: walk through the door
- C: Codex · B: Kept blades (destroy them here) · Q: Quest log · T: Travel between the towns of every realm you have reached
- Battle: 1–4 strike, S summon, G guard, P potion, F fists, Break a blade (free action)

## What's in Chapter I

- 5 lands: Brightmeadow Village, Whisperwood, Stonebrow Hills, the Quiet Graves, Ember Lair
- 5 battles: Goblin Scout, Kobold Trapper, Owlbear, Gelatinous Cube, Young Red Dragon
- 9 side quests, 12 villagers, 10 enterable buildings, 7+ treasure chests
- Every Codex entry has its own sword design
- Monster blades stay with you but gather curses
- Morvane's Soulgems bring fallen blades back, at a price: take 8 and you become a dragon (the alternate ending). Elder Maren can purify you, but only once. Come back too close to a dragon after that and the village banishes you: the Exile's run. Beat the dragon while exiled to kill the dragon inside you, lift the banishment, and face every monster again at double strength

## Chapter II: The Saltmarsh Coast

Reached by finishing Chapter I with the hero's ending and setting sail from Elder Maren.

- 5 new lands: Saltmarsh Harbor, Fogmire, the Drowned Halls, Grimtooth Warren, Galecrest Keep
- 5 new battles: Bog Hag, Drowned Knight, Goblin War-Chief, Kraken, Storm Wyvern
- 10 new characters, 7 side quests, 6 enterable buildings, coins and Marigold's Shop
- Granny Gristle's Hexbrews: a big boost for your next battle, and a permanent hex. Drink 6 and you become a goblin (the Goblin's Ending). Old Tobias's lighthouse can burn the hexes away, once
- Every chest now opens a treasure window with potions, coins and a unique Codex treasure

## Chapter III: The Frostspire Peaks

Reached by finishing Chapter II and sailing north with Captain Brine.

- 5 new lands: Hearthhold, Frostpine Wood, Glimmerdeep Caverns, Emberforge Ruins, Skyspire Citadel
- 5 new battles: Winter Wolf, Yeti, Stone Golem, Beholder, and the Frost Lich (strike it down 3 times to crack its soul gem, break the gem in battle in the Crystal Grotto, then kill the Lich for real)
- 9 new characters, 6 side quests, 8 enterable buildings, Brunhild's forge shop
- Vesper's Mirror Shards: summon something a second time, at a cost. Take 5 and your reflection walks away with your life (the Mirror's Ending). Sister Ysolde's Dawn Bell can ring the glass out of you, once

## Chapter IV: The Shattered Vale

Reached by finishing Chapter III and following the falling blades south.

- The blades you let crumble never turned to dust. The Hollow King collected and corrupted them.
- 5 new lands: Ashfall Camp, Rustwood, the Field of a Thousand Swords, the Hollow Mines, the Blade Spire
- 5 new battles: Rust Knight, Blade Swarm, Shadow Summoner, Iron Hydra, and the Hollow King, who calls up corrupted copies of the people and creatures you have summoned (at half health) that you must defeat to reach him
- Corrupted copies of your own old swords wait on the Field. Free them and they come back Redeemed (summonable again, 25% stronger)
- Kept blades with 3 or more curses can turn on you in battle
- The Whispering Blade's pacts make your swords sharper. Make 5 and you become a sword (the Blade's Ending). Osric the Blind Monk can unmake them, once
- Garret's wagon shop, 5 side quests

## Chapter V: The Celestial Reaches

- Climb the ladder of light from Ashfall Camp to a realm of floating islands: Cloudhaven, the Windmill Isles, Starfall Crater, the Clockwork Archive and the Throne of First Light.
- Five battles: Storm Giant (thunderclap), Couatl (heals), Djinni (wishes), the Clockwork Dragon (beat it and its inventor, **the Tinkerer**, climbs out for round two: overcharged zaps, cog shields, repair kits and clockwork wasps), and **The Unwritten**, which erases a sword every 3 turns and rewrites the dragon, wyvern, lich and Hollow King as echoes you must beat again.
- Temptation: **Twinkle the fallen star** grants wishes (next foe starts at half health, all summons free) for 15 max HP each. Five wishes: the Star ending, where you become a constellation. Vela the Astronomer can cure you once.
- New side quests: turn 4 windmills, gather 5 stardust, return 5 lost books. Nimbus runs the cloud shop.

## Hidden places and rare creatures

- Every realm hides **2 secret places** (a hollow tree, a cracked rock, a wooden hatch, an ice cave, a rift, a swirl in the clouds). Walk into them.
- Every realm has **2 rare creatures** (marked ★) that wander their land, plus one guarding a secret place. 15 in all. They escape after 8 turns. Beat one for coins, a potion, a rare Codex entry, and the creature itself.

## Quest chains

Each realm has a 3-part chain where one reward unlocks the next part:

- I: Rowan → Bram → Elder Maren: the Old King's Sword
- II: Nella → Quill → Admiral Vane: the Golden Cutlass
- III: Kael → Tink → Thane Durin: the Frost Bow
- IV: Tamsin → Lyra → Osric: Lyra's Blade
- V: Auren → Gears → Auren: the Star Quill, which also weakens the Unwritten by 25%

## The twist, and Chapter VI: The Unraveled World

- When you beat the Unwritten, an old man made of light is left behind: the **real Auren**. The Unwritten was a shell that trapped him. Through it, something called the **Unraveler** pulled the strings of every monster. Every alternate ending that sent you back to the start was the Unraveler making Auren rewrite you. The game counts how many times this has happened.
- The ghost "Auren" in Cloudhaven was the Unraveler all along. Confront it, and a cutscene plays: it becomes a huge purple horned genie, turns the world inside out, and tries to kill you. The real Auren takes the hit and bonds with you.
- **Chapter VI** takes place in a twisted mirror of all five lands: flipped upside down, with inverted colors and backwards names. You free them in reverse order (the Reaches, the Vale, the Frostspire, the coast, Brightmeadow), each with **five twisted guardians** that have familiar names and much more power.
- **Auren** floats beside you the whole time. Press **H** (or the Auren button) and he tells you the truth, including where the villagers lied.
- **Villagers** are passive-aggressive and give you wrong directions. Five of them hand you a quest and then attack you as **Hollow villagers**. **Shadows** drift toward you and hurt you if they touch you.
- **Allies:** Morvane, Granny Gristle, Vesper and the Whispering Blade help you **for free** here: a clean soulgem, a brew with no hex, a free mirror shard, or curses cleansed. Each is available once after every victory.
- **20 backward side quests** from allies, lying villagers and the **quest board** in every town: you plant things back into glowing mounds or gather things up. For example, you un-cook Hilda's stew by planting the toadstool, daisy and thornbush back in their lands.
- You can't leave the Unraveled World. You can only travel between its towns you have freed.
- **Final battle:** the Unraveler unravels a sword every 4 turns, and it pulls a Twisted Dragon, a Twisted Hollow King and Your Own Shadow out of your past. Auren's light heals you every 5 turns. Beat it for the **True Ending** cutscene, then go home to Brightmeadow, where the real Auren is waiting.

## Arena battles

Battles take place in an arena painted from the land you are in: sky at the back, an arc of that land's own trees and scenery around an oval clearing, hills on the sides. The monster stands large at the far side and your knight (or both knights) stand at the near side, each with a health bar. Along the bottom is a brick bar holding the **sword slots** (Player 1 on the left, Player 2 on the right); click a slab to strike with that sword. Every attack is animated: your summoned sword spins through the air and slashes the monster (fists throw a punch), with a damage number. Monsters attack by element: fire breath, ice shards, magic or holy beams, shadow orbs, water splashes, lashing vines, or claw marks. Guarding shows a shield glow and potions show healing sparkles. On a knight's turn a pop-up menu above the bar holds Summon, Guard, Potion, Fists and the other actions. During the monster's turn the menu hides so you can watch.

## Two players

- Press **2 Players** in the top bar to add a second player on the same screen. Player 1 (red) uses the **arrow keys**; Player 2 (blue) uses **W A S D**. Press **1 Player** to go back.
- When you are close, you share one view. There is no limit on how far apart you can go. Walk far apart and the screen **splits in two** (left/right or top/bottom), each half following one knight. Walk back together and it joins up again. Each knight goes in and out of houses and buildings **on their own**; when you are in different places the screen is always split (Player 1 on the left, Player 2 on the right). If Player 1 travels to another land, Player 2 comes along. Player 2 can talk to people, open chests, read signs and start fights.
- **Battle keys.** Player 1 (right hand): J K L strike with slots 1–3, U summon, I guard, O potion, H fists, Y break a blade. Player 2 (left hand): 1 2 3 strike, Q summon, E guard, R potion, F fists, T break. In the Summon list and other pop-ups, Player 1 moves with the arrow keys and Enter, Player 2 with W A S D and Space. Solo: 1–3, S, G, P, F, B.
- In battle you take turns. Whoever **started the fight goes first**, then the other player, then the monster attacks one of you at random. Both knights' health and sword bars are on screen at once; the waiting knight's panel is shown above or below the active one. Each player has their own health, sword slots, guard and free summon. Kept blades are split between you. The Codex is shared, so each thing can still be summoned only once.
- When a fight starts and the other knight is not right there, a quick cutscene shows them **running to the fight** by the shortest path (out of a building and across the land if needed). After the battle both knights stand side by side where it happened. The same run plays before any trip you must take together (ships, the crack, the ladder, new chapters).
- Monsters have **4× health** in two-player battles.
- If one of you falls, the other fights on alone. If both fall, you lose the battle.

## Getting around

- **Walking:** the crack in the world joins the far east of the Frostspire Peaks to Ashfall Camp. The ladder of light joins Ashfall Camp to Cloudhaven.
- **Sailing:** Brightmeadow ↔ Saltmarsh and Saltmarsh ↔ Hearthhold are sea routes, so they use fast travel.
- **Fast travel** works only while you stand in a start town (Brightmeadow, Saltmarsh, Hearthhold, Ashfall Camp, Cloudhaven).
- Every journey plays a short cutscene for each leg (boat, ice ship, crack in the world, ladder of light). Press Space to skip.

## Linked realms

- Some side quests are given in one realm and finished in another (earlier or later chapters)
- Travel only links the towns: Brightmeadow Village, Saltmarsh Harbor, Hearthhold and Ashfall Camp
